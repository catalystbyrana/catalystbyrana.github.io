About Mrs. Rana

A lower hill to the same mark.

Mrs. Rana is a teacher in the UAE and the founder of Catalyst.

She builds every course herself, from the school textbook, in the order lessons are taught. Each page keeps only what earns marks. Each figure is drawn to match the one in the book, because that is the figure the exam asks you to label. Each answer is checked before a student sees it.

The name comes from chemistry. A catalyst does not change where a reaction ends; it lowers the hill the reaction has to climb. That is the promise of every page here: the same full marks, with less effort to get there.

“If it doesn't help you in the exam, it doesn't go on the page.”
